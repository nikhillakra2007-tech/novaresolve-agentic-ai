# Project Rules & Persistent Instructions

## 1. Frontend Development Standard — Scroll-Craft
- Whenever working on the frontend (Next.js, React, HTML/CSS/JS, landing pages, or UI views), **ALWAYS** use the `scroll-craft` skill (embedded in `.agents/skills/scroll-craft` and global `~/.gemini/config/skills/scroll-craft`).
- Adhere to the scroll-craft methodology:
  - Scroll as timeline and dimensional layering (independently moving background, subject, foreground, atmospheric planes).
  - High variety of device families, token-driven design floor, semantic HTML, restrained motion, and responsive mobile art direction.
  - Avoid generic/flat/low-poly template defaults.
